Bench test · display + knob

RetroRadio wiring

Wire the 32×8 LED marquee to the HiFi-ESP32 through two XC4486 level converters. Use only the TXI → TXO pins on the converters: TXI is the 3.3V side, TXO the 5V side. The RXI/RXO pins only step down and won't work here. Pin order on the real modules differs from the drawing, so go by the printed labels.

Signals

Three data lines leave the board at 3.3V, get lifted to 5V by the converters, and drive the matrices. The clock is shared by both matrices.

CLK (shared) MOSI → left SDA MISO → right SDA Matrix SCL = clock, SDA = data (AIP1640, not real I2C)

Power and ground

The HUSB238 feeds 5V to the matrices and the converters' HV side. The board supplies 3.3V to the LV side. Every ground joins together.

5V from HUSB238 3.3V from board common ground ● joined  ·  a break in a line = wires cross, not joined

Knob

The Jaycar XC3736 encoder module plugs onto female jumper leads, which are joined to wires soldered to the pads on the back of the board. Power the module from 3.3V, not 5V: its on-board pull-ups would otherwise put 5V on the ESP32's pins. The firmware also enables the ESP32's internal pull-ups, which covers the SW pin. One click is one full quadrature cycle (ENC_STEPS_PER_DETENT 4). Verified working 2026-11-20.

CLK → IO33 DT → IO27 SW → IO21 + → 3V3 − → GND Clockwise turns volume down? Set ENC_REVERSE 1

Before you power up

LV is 3.3V, HV is 5V. If they're swapped, 5V can feed back into the ESP32's pins.
One ground for everything. The HUSB238 − and the board's header GND must be joined, or the display will show garbage.
Power-up order: charger first, then the board. If the board is on first, its 3.3V leaks onto the 5V line through the converters (about 2.5V). The USB-C charger then refuses to switch on, and the matrix runs dim on the leak. In the finished radio the charger feeds everything, so this only matters on the bench.
The printed LV/HV labels are correct. Ignore the online review that says they're reversed.
