Scenes

    What to look for

    • Compact caps fits about 8 characters on screen, against about 5 with the classic font. Switch with the buttons above (on the radio it's in Settings).
    • Station change: burst of static, the tuning needle sweeps to the station's place in the list, then the name drops in and bounces.
    • Browse rolls like a slot-machine reel: up for clockwise, down for anticlockwise. The bottom row shows where you are in the list.
    • Lists: with two or more lists, a "◂ LISTS" entry sits between the last station and the first. Press it to pick a list.
    • Standby picks the cat or the frog at random each time, and the scene follows Perth time: sunrise and a yawn (5:30–9 am), a moth or a fly to hunt (9 am–5:30 pm), moon and stars with grooming or croaking (5:30–9 pm), then fast asleep. The preview runs through all four times of day for each. On wake the creature hops, then the station tunes in.
    • Setup hotspot loops through the WiFi name and address, and changes to "CONNECTED / NOW OPEN 192.168.4.1" once a phone joins.
